CPG companies sit on more data than almost any industry on earth. Six or more parallel streams — POS, media spend, retail audit, pricing, market research, share data — pour into the business every week. And yet, decisions still get made on Tuesday's deck instead of Tuesday's data.

## The Problem Isn't Data Scarcity

It is data fragmentation. Each stream has its own owner, its own cadence, its own definition of a "product," and its own dashboard. The brand manager looking at next quarter's allocation is reconciling six versions of the truth in her head, in real time, every week.

> "The bottleneck is not data. It is the absence of a system that turns six fragmented streams into one decision."

The fix is not another dashboard. It is an integrated decision system — a layer that joins, reconciles, and serves the six streams to whoever needs them, with the business logic and the model embedded in the pipe.
